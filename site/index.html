The sheet demo and uploaded OBJ/self-contained GLB triangle meshes run through the cloth-lab Rust core compiled to WebAssembly. Mesh density changes the sheet topology while preserving its physical size. Textile presets map to solver compliance and contact parameters; solver controls change the explicit fixed-step configuration. Pins are persistent kinematic constraints whose targets can be moved. The inspector reads authoritative topology, constraint, error, contact, and normalized-asset evidence from Rust; wireframe and normal overlays are rendering-only views of those authoritative positions and triangles. Uploaded mesh formats still provide geometry only: seams, patterns, measured fabric properties, skins, and animation are not inferred when unsupported by the source.

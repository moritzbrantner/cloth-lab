The sheet demo and uploaded OBJ/self-contained GLB triangle meshes run through the cloth-lab Rust core compiled to WebAssembly. Mesh density changes the sheet topology while preserving its physical size. Textile presets map to solver compliance and contact parameters; solver controls change the explicit fixed-step configuration. Pins are persistent kinematic constraints whose targets can be moved. Collision scenery is an explicit validated Rust input: it can be disabled or configured as a sphere or x-axis capsule, with accessible position/size controls and direct pointer dragging. Camera orbit, zoom, and pan remain renderer-only inspection state; cloth, pin, and obstacle dragging use the same current camera basis so view changes do not alter interaction meaning. The inspector reads authoritative topology, constraint, error, contact, obstacle, and normalized-asset evidence from Rust; wireframe and normal overlays are rendering-only views of authoritative positions and triangles. Uploaded mesh formats still provide geometry only: seams, patterns, measured fabric properties, skins, and animation are not inferred when unsupported by the source.
